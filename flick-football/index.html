Privacy Policy — Flick Football

Last updated: November 4, 2026

Overview

Flick Football is a single-player and local pass-and-play trick-shot game. This policy explains what data the app collects — which is none.

Data collection

Flick Football does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no tracking, and no ads.

Local storage

The app may save your game settings (difficulty, scene) and local leaderboard scores on your device. This data never leaves your device.

Network access

Flick Football does not request internet access and makes no network connections.

Third-party links

The app contains no outside links or third-party services.

Children's privacy

The app does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to this policy

Any updates will be posted on this page with a revised date.

Contact

Questions can be sent to me@damon1974.com.
